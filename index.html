PERSONAL GOOGLE WORKSPACE UTILITY

Codex Personal

A personal desktop utility operated by Denis Simankov to work with his Google Drive and Gmail accounts through Google APIs.

What it does

  • Lists and searches accessible Drive files and folders and reads selected files when requested.
  • Searches and reads email messages and prepares replies.
  • Sends email only when the account owner explicitly requests it.

Google access is authorized by the account owner using OAuth. The utility is intended for the owner's personal use; it is not a public email or file management service.

When the owner requests AI assistance, selected email or file content may be supplied to the AI service used for that task. Google credentials are kept on the owner's computer and are not published on this website.
