Codex Personal

Privacy policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Codex Personal is a personal desktop utility operated by Sergei Boaga. This policy describes its intended handling of Google account data.

Data accessed and purpose

With the account owner's OAuth authorization, the utility accesses Google Drive file and folder metadata and selected file contents to list, find and read documents. It accesses Gmail message headers, bodies and selected attachments to search, summarize and prepare replies. Sending email requires an explicit instruction from the owner. Access is limited to the permissions granted during authorization.

Storage and security

OAuth client settings and access or refresh tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer, outside this public website and repository. The website does not receive these credentials, emails or Drive files. Any local downloads, extracts or reports are retained on the owner's computer until deleted by the owner.

AI assistance and disclosure

When the owner asks an AI assistant to process a message or document, content needed for that task may be transmitted to the chosen AI provider, including OpenAI when using Codex. That processing is governed by the provider's terms, privacy policy and the owner's account settings. The operator does not sell Google account data or use it for advertising, and does not use it to train general-purpose AI models.

Retention and control

The owner may delete local data and credentials and revoke authorization through the Google Account's third-party connections settings. Revocation stops further authorized access; it does not delete copies already stored locally or in AI conversations. Those copies must be removed using the relevant local or service controls.

Google API user data

Codex Personal's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

This website

These informational pages have no application login, analytics scripts, forms or advertising. They are hosted by GitHub Pages; GitHub may process standard hosting request information under its own privacy statement.
